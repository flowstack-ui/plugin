# Privacy Policy

The FLOWSTACK UI plugin contains workflow skills and configures the public
FLOWSTACK MCP service at `https://agents.brick-ui.com/mcp`. When a host invokes
that service, it sends the tool name and bounded arguments needed for the
request, such as an exact public package version, guide or component ID, search
query, or structured public composition check. Do not include credentials,
private source, personal data, or customer material in those arguments.

The MCP service is read-only and locked to public FLOWSTACK package guidance.
It does not use cookies or session identifiers, does not provide authentication,
and does not intentionally retain request content or collect product telemetry.
Infrastructure providers may process ordinary connection metadata needed to
operate and secure the service. The host product also processes data under its
own terms and privacy policy.

Security or privacy concerns may be reported through the repository security
policy.
